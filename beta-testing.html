Help shape DJ Air

Beta Testers Wanted

We're looking for a small group of enthusiastic, experienced broadcasters to help make Rivendell DJ Air more reliable, practical and enjoyable to use. Up to five beta tester places are available.

This is a limited, invitation-only testing program. DJ Air is still under development, and no public installer has been released.

Who we're looking for

RIVENDELL USERS & ENGINEERS

People who operate or support Rivendell and can evaluate installation, playout, audio routing and real-world broadcasting workflows.

INTERNET & SMALL-MARKET RADIO

Internet radio stations, smaller broadcast operations, station operators and engineering leads are especially welcome.

DIFFERENT AUDIO HARDWARE

Testers with varied sound cards, including AudioScience hardware, and different Linux and studio setups can help us understand compatibility.

PIPEWIRE & JACK

PipeWire is our primary development focus. Traditional JACK compatibility matters too, but has not yet been officially validated; testers with JACK experience are particularly valuable.

What beta testers will help with

REAL-WORLD FUNCTIONALITY

Try live on-air controls, remote microphone workflows, voice tracking, audio library tools, bulk uploads, administration and other features as test builds become available.

DESIGN & USABILITY

Tell us what looks right, what feels awkward and how the interface could be clearer, faster or more accessible.

FEATURE IDEAS & IMPROVEMENTS

Suggest useful capabilities and help prioritize refinements that matter to working broadcasters.

ONGOING TEST BUILDS

Help check fixes and new features as updated builds become available. Updates may arrive daily during active development or several days apart.

Special interest: audio meter accuracy

We would especially appreciate hearing from someone experienced with audio meter ballistics, peak indication and standardized average-level measurement. We'd like to refine the DJ Air meters to provide meaningful, responsive indications of average and peak levels while respecting practical measurement and display limitations.

Direct communication matters

This is a hands-on collaboration, not a large public beta. We're looking for people who can respond reasonably promptly, exchange detailed feedback by email and participate in occasional scheduled phone conversations when troubleshooting or discussing improvements. A willingness to stay engaged through successive test builds is important.

Applicants should be prepared to describe their station or test environment, Linux distribution, Rivendell version, audio hardware and whether they use PipeWire or JACK.

Interested in joining?

We are preparing a dedicated project contact address and application instructions. They will be added here before applications open. Please check back soon.

Tester selection will be limited to five participants. Applying does not guarantee a place; test builds will be shared privately with selected testers when ready.
