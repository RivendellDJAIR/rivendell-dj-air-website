Community Edition

Community Features

Rivendell DJ Air Community Edition is the free edition, focused on browser-based Live On Air presentation and voice tracking for Rivendell stations.

Community Edition application areas

LIVE ON AIR COMMUNITY

Browser-based presenter controls for Rivendell playout, including on-air status, timing information and the Live Presenter workflow.

VOICE TRACKING COMMUNITY

A dedicated workspace for recording and arranging voice tracks alongside programme audio, with local draft recording and preview workflows.

Development & verification

Community Edition functionality is undergoing release testing and verification. Some workflows may change before public release.

IN VERIFICATION

VOICE TRACKING COMMUNITY REFINEMENTS

Recording, preview and playback reliability are being validated for the Community Edition workflow.

Community Edition does not include the Pro Audio Library or Pro-only administration and engineering tools. Voice tracks can be saved as private local drafts; native publishing to Rivendell is not enabled by default. Public installer availability and verified feature support will be announced on the Downloads page after acceptance testing.
