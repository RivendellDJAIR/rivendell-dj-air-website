Rivendell DJ Air

Radio broadcasting tools.
One connected workspace.

A web-based companion for Rivendell, bringing live on-air operation, voice tracking, audio library workflows and administration into a consistent interface.

Explore DJ Air

Explore the main broadcasting tools and supporting station-management interfaces. Availability depends on the installed version.

LIVE ON AIR

Browser-based presenter controls, including remote microphone operation and live playout interaction.

VOICE TRACKING

Record, edit, position and publish voice tracks within your broadcasting workflow.

AUDIO LIBRARY

Manage uploads and audio preparation, including normalization and undo workflows.

ADMINISTRATION

Manage application access and configuration through the DJ Air administration interface.

ACCESSIBILITY

Presenter display preferences for text size, contrast, magnification and reduced motion.

BULK AUDIO UPLOADER

Import multiple audio files with destination group selection and per-file progress tracking.

BACKUP & RESTORE

Application and station backup interfaces. Expanded storage destinations and safe database procedures are in development.

ENGINEERING DIAGNOSTICS

System health, service and audio-routing observations, with additional verified measurements in development.
