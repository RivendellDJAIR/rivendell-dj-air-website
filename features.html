Features

A closer look at the four Rivendell DJ Air modules.

LIVE ON AIR

Presenter-facing browser controls for live broadcasting, including remote microphone operation and music ducking.

VOICE TRACKING

Record voice audio, edit timing and placement, preview changes, save drafts and publish to the connected Rivendell workflow.

AUDIO LIBRARY

Upload and organize audio, review normalization operations and use supported undo functions.

ADMINISTRATION

Manage authorized access and application-level configuration.

Feature behavior depends on the installed version and its supported Rivendell environment. Release documentation will accompany the approved installer.
