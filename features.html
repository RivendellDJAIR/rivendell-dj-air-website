Features

Rivendell DJ Air brings browser-based presentation, voice tracking, audio management and station administration together. Capabilities vary by installed build; features still undergoing development or verification are identified separately below.

Core application areas

LIVE ON AIR

Browser-based presenter controls for Rivendell playout, with remote microphone workflows, on-air status and timing information.

VOICE TRACKING

A dedicated workspace for recording and arranging voice tracks alongside programme audio, with preview and editing workflows.

AUDIO LIBRARY

Audio uploads, library management, loudness analysis and gain-based normalization workflows, including supported undo operations.

ADMINISTRATION

Access controls, application configuration, backup and restore interfaces, and system health and diagnostics tools.

In development & verification

The following improvements are being worked on or validated. They should not be considered available in a public release until testing and release approval are complete.

IN DEVELOPMENT

BACKUP DESTINATIONS

Multiple administrator-selected destinations for local disks, removable drives and mounted network storage. Cloud-provider configuration is planned as an extensible option.

IN DEVELOPMENT

ENGINEERING DIAGNOSTICS

Verified component health, audio-routing observations, and available PipeWire and WebRTC measurements, with actionable troubleshooting information and support packages.

IN DEVELOPMENT

VOICE TRACKING REFINEMENTS

Outgoing and incoming song waveform previews, independent incoming-song preview control, and improved recording and playback reliability.

IN VERIFICATION

MOBILE EXPERIENCE

Responsive navigation, clock placement and controls across the application, including the Audio Library page.

This is a development overview, not a compatibility or release guarantee. Public installer availability and verified feature support will be announced on the Downloads page after acceptance testing.
